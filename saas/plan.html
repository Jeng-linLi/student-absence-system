MVP Roadmap, Timeline & Cost Estimation MVP 路線圖、時程與成本估算

Three phases over 12 months, then steady-state enhancement. 十二個月內分三期推進，其後進入常態優化。

Governance principle（治理原則）: AI may assist but never decide an absence outcome. Every AI suggestion is logged with model version, input hash, and the human decision that followed; students may always request human-only handling. AI 只能輔助，不得決定請假結果。每項 AI 建議均記錄模型版本、輸入雜湊與其後的人工決定；學生可隨時要求全程人工處理。

6. Recommended Next Steps建議後續行動

  1. Week 0 — Approve. Steering committee approves MVP budget (¥1.0M) and names the product owner. 第 0 週 — 核准。指導委員會批准 MVP 預算（100 萬）並指定產品負責人。
  2. Week 1 — Policy workshop. Registry confirms thresholds, SLA, routing; ITO provisions Azure subscription and Entra ID app registration. 第 1 週 — 政策工作坊。教務處確認門檻、SLA 與路由；資訊科技處開通 Azure 訂閱與 Entra ID 應用註冊。
  3. Week 2 — Data & security review. DPO approves classification, retention and region. 第 2 週 — 資料與安全評審。資料保護負責人核准資料分級、保存期與存放區域。
  4. Week 3 — Build starts. Two-week sprints with a fortnightly demo to the steering committee. 第 3 週 — 開發啟動。以兩週為一個衝刺，每兩週向指導委員會展示成果。
  5. Week 11 — Pilot. Two programmes (~600 students) go live in parallel with the existing email process. 第 11 週 — 試點。兩個項目（約 600 名學生）上線，與現行電郵流程並行。
  6. Week 14 — Go / no-go. Adoption and CSAT thresholds decide university-wide rollout. 第 14 週 — 推行決策。以採用率與滿意度門檻決定是否全校推行。
