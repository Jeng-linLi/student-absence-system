Governance principle（治理原則）: AI may assist but never decide an absence outcome. Every AI suggestion is logged with model, prompt and policy versions, input hash, abstention state, and the human decision that followed; students may always request human-only handling. A feature flag must return the whole system to deterministic rules without redeployment. AI 只能輔助，不得決定請假結果。每項建議均記錄模型、提示詞與政策版本、輸入雜湊、拒答狀態及其後的人工決定；學生可隨時要求全程人工處理。特徵開關必須能在不重部署的情況下，讓系統回到確定性規則。

5.2 AI ethics, rights & accountabilityAI 倫理、權利與問責

Compliance is not ethics. A signed DPIA means the system may lawfully proceed — not that a student feels fairly treated. Three education-specific conditions make a generic AI-ethics list insufficient. 合規不等於倫理。DPIA 簽核只代表可以合法地做，不代表學生覺得被公平對待。教育場景有三個特殊性，通用 AI 倫理清單不足以因應。

Power asymmetry權力不對等

A student asking their own advisor for leave cannot easily say "no AI please". Opting out must be silent, reason-free, equally fast and never recorded for analytics. 學生很難對自己的導師說「我不要 AI 參與」。拒絕必須無痕、免理由、不降速、不影響結果，且不得留下可供分析的標記。

Irreversible records資料不可逆

Medical certificates, family and religious observance cannot be recalled once leaked or labelled. Raw reasons and attachments never enter a model; a label must never feed back into the next decision. 病假證明、家庭與宗教節慶一旦外流或被打上標籤就收不回來。原始事由與附件不進模型；標籤不得回饋到下一次決定。

Procedure is access程序即受教權

Leave outcomes affect attendance, assessment, make-up exams, scholarships and visas — an "advisory" hint can still change a result. Appeals go to someone other than the original approver. 請假結果牽連出席、考評、補考、獎助與簽證，「僅供參考」也可能改寫結果；申訴須由非原審批人處理。

Ethics veto（倫理否決）: an independent ethics review (including student representation, no supplier conflict) can block every H0–H3 transition without proposing a technical alternative; DPIA sign-off does not substitute for it. Consent is not a blanket authorisation for prohibited uses. Restoration means putting the student back where they would have been — the burden of proof sits with the institution, never the student. Full framework and the checkbox acceptance list: AI-ETHICS.md. 獨立的倫理審查（含學生代表、供應商無利益衝突）可否決 H0–H3 每一次升級，無須提出技術替代方案；DPIA 簽核不能取代。同意不是禁止用途的萬用授權。復權是把學生放回「若無此事件應有」的狀態，舉證責任在學校，不在學生。完整框架與可勾選驗收表見 AI-ETHICS.md。

6. Recommended Next Steps建議後續行動

  1. Week 0 — Approve. Steering committee approves MVP budget (¥1.0M) and names the product owner. 第 0 週 — 核准。指導委員會批准 MVP 預算（100 萬）並指定產品負責人。
  2. Week 1 — Policy workshop. Registry confirms thresholds, SLA, routing; ITO provisions Azure subscription and Entra ID app registration. 第 1 週 — 政策工作坊。教務處確認門檻、SLA 與路由；資訊科技處開通 Azure 訂閱與 Entra ID 應用註冊。
  3. Week 2 — Data & security review. DPO approves classification, retention and region. 第 2 週 — 資料與安全評審。資料保護負責人核准資料分級、保存期與存放區域。
  4. Week 3 — Build starts. Two-week sprints with a fortnightly demo to the steering committee. 第 3 週 — 開發啟動。以兩週為一個衝刺，每兩週向指導委員會展示成果。
